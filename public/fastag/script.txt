Ye sticker ek passive RFID tag hai.
Isme paisa nahi hota, sirf ek unique ID, gaadi ke aadhaar number jaisi.
Lekin battery ke bina ye bolta kaise hai?
Toll lane ke upar ek reader antenna lagi hoti hai.
Wo radio waves bhejta hai, aur wahi waves tag ko power de dete hain.
Tag jaag jaata hai, aur apni ID wapas bhej deta hai. Milliseconds mein.
