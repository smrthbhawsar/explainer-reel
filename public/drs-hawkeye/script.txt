DRS mein stumps pe lagti ball — kabhi wahan pahunchi hi nahi.
Ball toh pad pe ruk gayi thi. Phir woh red line aayi kahan se?
Yahin aata hai Hawk-Eye.
Stadium ke chaaron taraf, chhe high-speed cameras lage hote hain.
Har camera second mein sau se zyada frames leta hai, aur har frame mein dhoondta hai — ball.
Lekin ek camera akela kaafi nahi.
Ek camera sirf direction batata hai, distance nahi.
Do cameras alag angles se ball dekhte hain — jahan dono lines milti hain, wahi asli position.
Ise kehte hain triangulation.
Har frame ki position jodo, aur ball ka poora 3D path ban jaata hai — release se bounce tak, bounce se pad tak.
Ab asli twist.
Pad ke baad ball ka koi data hi nahi hota.
Toh software physics lagata hai — speed, swing, bounce ka angle — aur path ko stumps tak extend karta hai.
Wahi dashed line hai, prediction.
Phir teen sawaal. Pitching in line? Impact in line? Wickets hitting?
Agar ball stumps ko bas chhoo rahi ho, toh faisla field umpire ka rehta hai — Umpire's Call.
Agli baar woh red line dekho — woh replay nahi, ek prediction hai.
