Chai wale ko paanch sau rupaye UPI se bheje.
Paise kat gaye, par screen pe likha hai — Pending.
Kyun?
Kyunki ye ek payment nahi, ek relay race hai.
Tumhari app, tumhara bank, chai wale ka bank — sab baton pass karte hain.
Aur beech mein baitha hai NPCI — National Payments Corporation of India.
Simple bhasha mein: NPCI ek central switch hai, jo har bank ko har bank se jodta hai.
Pehle, tumhari app chai wale ki VPA, yaani UPI ID, NPCI ko bhejti hai.
NPCI pata lagata hai ki ye ID kis bank account se judi hai.
Phir tumhare bank ko debit ka order jaata hai, aur chai wale ke bank ko credit ka.
Har payment ko milta hai ek unique Transaction ID.
Ab agar chai wale ka bank time pe jawab na de, to timeout.
Paise kat chuke, par credit confirm nahi — isliye Pending.
Aur wahi Transaction ID retry pe double charge hone se bachata hai — ise kehte hain idempotency.
System status check karta hai — paise ya to pahunch jaate hain, ya automatic refund.
Har din saath karod se zyada transactions, aur har ek ka poora hisaab.
