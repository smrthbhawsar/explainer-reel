ChatGPT se apne company ke kal ke sales report ke baare mein poocho 
wo ya to mana kar dega, ya koi galat number bana kar de dega. 
Kyun? 
Kyunki har LLM ek closed-book exam ki tarah hota hai. 
Use sirf wahi yaad hai jo usne training ke waqt padha tha. 
Tumhara private ya naya data uske paas hai hi nahi. 
Yahin aata hai RAG — Retrieval-Augmented Generation. 
Simple bhasha mein: AI ko closed-book ki jagah open-book exam dene dena. 
Ye do hisson mein kaam karta hai.  
Pehle, tum apne PDFs, docs, aur databases ko chhote chunks mein tod kar Vector Database mein store karte ho. 
Ye AI ki personal library hai. 
Jab tum sawal poochte ho, system pehle is library se sirf relevant pages retrieve karta hai. 
Phir un pages ko tumhare sawal ke saath jod kar LLM ko bhejta hai,
aur LLM exact, accurate jawab generate karta hai.
Na model ko mehnga re-train karne ki zaroorat, na hallucination ka darr. 
Bas sahi context do, aur AI expert ban jaata hai.
